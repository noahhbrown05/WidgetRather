## The comparables Noah named

### Houseparty (2016–2021)
- Launched Feb 2016. Teens and students adopted it early through word of mouth. Features: drop-in group video, in-app games (trivia, Uno), Snapchat integration. [ProductMint](https://productmint.com/what-happened-to-houseparty/), [Failory](https://www.failory.com/cemetery/houseparty)
- Epic Games bought it in 2019 (~$35M). It picked up 50M+ users early in the pandemic and was the #1 social app in 82 countries by May 2020. [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/what-happened-to-houseparty/)
- It declined once people could meet in person again, facing competition from Zoom, FaceTime, and Discord. Epic announced the shutdown Sept 9, 2021. [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/video-games/2021/09/09/houseparty-shut-down-epic-games-metaverse/)
- **Lesson:** it rode an external spike (lockdown) and never turned that spike into a lasting habit.

### YOLO (2019– )
- Anonymous Q&A that plugged into Snapchat (Snap Kit). Users posted a sticker to their Story, and friends sent anonymous questions. It hit #1 in the US App Store about a week after launch, with 26M downloads, ~70% of them in the US. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/05/08/download-yolo-app/), [TechCrunch 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/02/28/anonymous-snapchat-group-chat-yolo/)
- Nine months later it still had ~10M active users and raised $8M. Bullying concerns followed it throughout, the same pattern as Sarahah (removed from the app stores in 2018) and YikYak. [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90346978/anonymous-qa-app-yolo-is-a-hit-with-teens-but-bullying-concerns-linger)
- **Lesson:** distribution came through a platform teens already used (Snapchat Stories). Anonymity drove growth, but it also caused the safety problems.

## Other teen-social comparables worth studying

### tbh (2017) and Gas (2022), both by Nikita Bier
- tbh launched in a single Georgia high school (earliest semester start). Within 2 days, 40% of Georgia high schoolers had downloaded it. The rollout went school by school, geofenced, to create exclusivity. [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier), [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/nikita-bier-got-5m-users-in-2-months-with-a-0-marketing-budget-and-then-sold-his-business-to-facebook-for-30m-he-did-it-twice-b3e0cc1c68)
- Gas (Aug 2022): anonymous compliment polls about classmates. It reached 1M DAU in 2 months and passed TikTok in App Store rankings in Oct–Nov 2022. Discord bought it in Jan 2023 and shut it down in Nov 2023; the month before the shutdown it had only ~2,900 downloads. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/discord-kills-gas-anonymous-compliments-app-bought-nine-months-ago)
- Bier's playbook: show the core value within the first ~3 seconds, and target teens because invites per user drop about 20% for every year of age from 13 to 18. Durable consumer social is rare, a "black swan." [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier)
- **Lesson:** poll mechanics plus school-by-school launches can explode, but novelty decays fast. Retention has to be designed in from the start.

### BeReal
- One daily notification at a random time; everyone posts at the same moment. Voodoo bought it for ~$537M in June 2024 and added ads and brand feeds. DAU estimates vary widely by source (from ~3M to 20M+). [BeReal news](https://bereal.com/news/voodoo-acquires-bereal), [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/bereal-statistics/)
- **Lesson:** a synchronized daily drop creates a shared moment. This is the closest analogue to our question drop.

### Locket (widget-first, close friends)
- Photos from friends appear right on your home-screen widget. Per its July 2026 press release: DAU up 22% since Jan 1, 2026; a **51% DAU/MAU ratio**; the average teen has **14 friends** on it. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/locket-solidifies-itself-as-go-to-social-platform-for-teens-and-young-adults-302826786.html)
- **Lesson:** a widget can be the product for teens. Small, close circles retain well. (Note: the numbers are company-reported.)

### Airbuds (widget-first, music)
- A widget showing what friends are listening to. 15M+ downloads, 5M MAU, 1.5M DAU; raised $5M (Sept 2025). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/17/airbuds-is-the-music-social-network-apple-and-spotify-wish-they-had-built/), [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2025/09/18/social-music-app-airbuds-has-1-5m-daily-users-and-5m-of-funding/)
- **Lesson:** a second widget-first teen social app that worked. Widgets are a real distribution surface for this age group.

### Direct "would you rather" competitors (quick scan, needs a deeper look)
- "Would You Rather? Live Polls": compare your answer against 6M+ votes, split by demographics. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/would-you-rather-live-polls/id1352732673)
- RatherVote: one daily would-you-rather with live results (web). [rathervote.com](https://www.rathervote.com/)
- askUs: daily questions and polls about friends. A user has requested a widget. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/askus-whos-most-likely-to/id6446650376)

## What this suggests for positioning (for team discussion, not decided)

A draft framing: **BeReal's timing + Locket's surface + tbh/Gas's poll mechanic, but the questions are about hypotheticals, never about people.**

Why "about hypotheticals, never about people" matters:
1. Apple's App Store rules reject apps used mainly for random/anonymous chat or "hot-or-not" voting on real people (see `safety-and-legal.md`). Gas-style "who in your class is…" polls are a review risk.
2. YOLO and Sarahah show that anonymity plus teens leads to bullying, then bad press, then removal.
3. "Would you rather" is inherently low-stakes and debate-starting, which fits the "talk about it tomorrow at school" goal.

Retention risk (the graveyard pattern: Houseparty, Gas, YOLO's decline): plan retention mechanics from day one, and track week-1 retention as the north-star metric (Raroque, see `raroque-playbook.md`).
